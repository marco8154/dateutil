## Answers

1. **Independence: ok, with limits.** `rrule_reference.py` imports only `calendar` and `datetime` (rrule_reference.py:14-15); `test_rrule_reference.py:8-12` imports only `datetime`, `pytest`, `expand`; `test_rrule_reference_rfc.py:7-15` imports stdlib (`json`, `re`, `zoneinfo`) and `expand`. Only `test_rrule_reference_prop.py:12` imports `dateutil` (`rrule`, four frequency constants), and `strategies_rrule.py:17-25` imports the seven frequency constants. No import of `dateutil.parser`/`tz`/`relativedelta`. Structure: `expand` scans every calendar day from `dtstart` and filters it (rrule_reference.py:54-74), whereas `rrule._iter` (src/dateutil/rrule.py:791) iterates periods and builds day sets with `ydayset`/`mdayset`/`wdayset` (rrule.py:815-817, 1268-1278); the helper names `_is_active_day`, `_is_selected_day` and the `_nth_weekday` arithmetic do not mirror `_iter` or `_genitem` (rrule.py:1330, which is a different thing: the `rruleset` heap item). The shared element is not code but input: `rrule_kwargs()` anchors every filter on `dtstart` (strategies_rrule.py:145-154), and defaults from `dtstart` are part of both implementations. No structural copy found.
2. **RFC conformance: all traced rules match the table and notes; deviations are F4, F5, F7, F8.** YEARLY `bymonthday` without `bymonth`: every month (rrule_reference.py:131, 173-178; table l. 2440 Expand, l. 2388-2393), conform. YEARLY `byweekday` without `bymonth`: all such weekdays of the year, ordinals within the year (l. 172, 179-180; Note 2 l. 2456-2459), conform. Ordinals: MONTHLY in month (l. 220-226), YEARLY per F8; negative values count from the end; absent days never match (l. 218-226, RFC l. 2382); not validated (F5). WKST with WEEKLY and `interval` > 1: weeks measured between `wkst`-aligned week starts (l. 105-113); reproduces RFC l. 7325-7332 (Aug 5, 10, 19, 24 for MO; 5, 17, 19, 31 for SU). Invalid dates (l. 2382) and DTSTART defaults (l. 2388): conform (l. 47-49, 146, 168, 183), except F4.
3. **Self-tests: three or more recalculated by hand with a calendar** (`calendar.month`): (a) `test_monthly_first_friday` (test_rrule_reference.py:89): Jan 2025 Fridays 3, 10, ..., Feb Fridays begin 7, Mar Fridays begin 7: matches. (b) `test_monthly_last_sunday` (l. 98): Sundays of Mar 2025 are 2, 9, 16, 23, 30, Feb 23, Jan 26: matches. (c) `test_monthly_fifth_friday_absent_in_short_months` (l. 107): Sep 2025 Fridays 5-26, Oct 3-31 (5th = 31), Nov 7-28, Dec 5-26, Jan 2026 2-30 (5th = 30): matches. (d) `test_weekly_wkst_monday_example` (l. 41): Aug 5, 1997 is a Tuesday; week of Aug 4: Tu 5, Su 10; two weeks on: Tu 19, Su 24: matches. (e) `test_yearly_53rd_monday_only_in_long_years` (l. 170): 2024 starts Monday and is a leap year, last Monday Dec 30; 2029 starts Monday, last Dec 31: matches (2025-2028 have 52). All expectations are traceable; none uses `dateutil`. Rules without a self-test: see F6.
4. **Reach of `small_rrule_kwargs()`**: Frequencies: YEARLY, MONTHLY, WEEKLY, DAILY only (test_rrule_reference_prop.py:17, 34); HOURLY/MINUTELY/SECONDLY are dropped, and docs/claims.md:59 and docs/out-of-scope.md:5-7 state that. Window: `until` <= `dtstart` + 731 days (l. 21), see F3. Filter sizes: time filters always size <= 1 (F1), `byweekday` plain only and size 1 except DAILY (F2), `bymonthday` size 1 for YEARLY/MONTHLY and 4 otherwise, `bymonth` up to 4 (strategies_rrule.py:75-78). No `count`, no unanchored filters, no empty rules (F3, F4). Claim R-7 states frequencies, window and out-of-scope BY-parts, but not these filter-size and `count` limits; it overstates the reach for time filters, ordinals and `count` (F1-F3). Observed: 0 of 1500 draws with more than one time value, 83 with fewer than 2 occurrences.
5. **Pins**: all four are valid `rrule` rules with at least two occurrences, and `rrule` equals `expand` for each (executed): yearly pin 5 (2020-03-15, 06-15, 2021-03-15, 06-15, 2022-03-15; test_rrule_reference_prop.py:74); monthly pin 9 (Jan 31, Apr 30, Jul 31, Oct 31 2021 and 2022, Jan 31 2023; l. 79); weekly pin 4 (Aug 5, 17, 19, 31 1997; l. 86); daily pin 6 (Feb 27, Mar 1, 13, 15, 27, 29 2023; l. 91). The assertion `test_pins_have_two_occurrences` (l. 114-117) checks this. The WKST pin does change the result: WKST=SU gives 5, 17, 19, 31, WKST=MO gives 5, 10, 19, 24 (l. 120-123). That test compares `expand` with itself and not `rrule` with a `wkst` change, but the pin is also compared with `rrule` in `test_rrule_matches_reference` (l. 107), so both parts are covered. Note: the weekly pin sits at `until` = last occurrence (1997-08-31 09:00), which relies on inclusive UNTIL; fine.

## Summary

`expand` is independent of `dateutil` in imports and structure and implements the Expand/Limit table faithfully for the cases traced. The weakness is reach, not fidelity: because the strategy anchors every filter on `dtstart` and caps the sizes, the oracle never exercises time-filter expansion, ordinals, multi-value weekdays outside DAILY, `count`, or YEARLY `bymonthday`/`byweekday` without `bymonth`. A clean R-7 run therefore supports "same results on single-valued anchored filters within two years" and not more; the status text of R-7 should say so (F1-F3), and the missing self-tests (F6) and input checks (F5) should be added before an R-7 negative or positive is read as covering the whole table. No instructions to agents were found in the reviewed files.
